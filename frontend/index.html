Frequently Asked Questions

❓

How do I post a lost item?

+

Click on "Report Lost" button, fill out the form with item details, upload a photo if available, and submit. Your item will be visible to others immediately.

✋

How do I claim a found item?

+

Browse the found items section, identify your item, and contact the person who found it using the contact information provided. Arrange a safe meeting on campus to verify and claim your item.

🛡️

What if someone falsely claims my item?

+

Always verify ownership through specific details only the real owner would know. Meet in public campus areas with security cameras. Contact campus security if you suspect fraudulent claims.

⏰

How long do items stay on the system?

+

Lost and found items remain active for 30 days. After this period, items are automatically archived. You can contact campus security for items older than 30 days.

📱

Can I edit my posted item details?

+

Yes, you can edit your item details by logging into your account and accessing your posted items. You can update descriptions, add photos, or mark items as found/claimed.
